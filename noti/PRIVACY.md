# Privacy Policy for Noti

**Effective Date:** 2026/07/05

This Privacy Policy describes how Noti ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
Noti is a local desktop application. All your notes, stickers, and timer data are stored locally on your device using a local SQLite database. We do not operate any central servers, and we do not collect, harvest, or transmit your personal usage data, notes, or application metrics to ourselves or any third parties.

## 2. Google User Data and OAuth
Noti requires a Google Account for authentication and automatic data synchronization. When you log in, the Application will request access to your Google account via OAuth.

* **Exact Scope Requested:** The application requests the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata` scope.
* **What this means:** We only request access to a hidden, application-specific folder within your Google Drive (the appData folder). Noti cannot read, view, or modify any of your personal files, documents, or photos stored in your wider Google Drive.
* **How we use it:** The authentication token is stored locally and securely on your device. It is used strictly to automatically sync your local SQLite database with this hidden folder so your notes remain consistent and backed up across your devices.
* **Data Transfer:** Your data transfers directly between your desktop application and Google's servers. At no point does your Google User Data pass through or reside on any servers owned or operated by Noti's developer.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
Because your data is either stored locally on your device or directly within your personal Google Drive account, we have no access to your data. Therefore, we cannot and do not share, sell, or disclose your personal information or notes to any third parties.

## 4. Security
Your data's security relies on the security of your local operating system and your Google Account credentials. We recommend maintaining strong passwords and enabling two-factor authentication on your Google Account.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the Application repository, and the effective date will be updated accordingly.
